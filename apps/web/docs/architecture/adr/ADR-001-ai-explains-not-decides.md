# ADR-001: AI Explains Decisions. AI Does Not Make Decisions.

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-04

## Context

SmartPulse is an AI-assisted trading intelligence platform.

The objective is to help traders make better decisions, not to automate or replace their judgment.

Many AI trading products attempt to predict markets or tell users exactly when to buy or sell. This creates unrealistic expectations, increases legal and regulatory risk, and encourages poor trading discipline.

## Decision

SmartPulse will never make trading decisions for users.

Instead, SmartPulse will:

- Explain the current market context.
- Highlight opportunities that match the trader's profile.
- Explain why an opportunity exists.
- Recommend preparation and risk considerations.
- Encourage disciplined execution.

The trader remains responsible for every trading decision.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Encourages disciplined trading.
- Builds long-term user trust.
- Reduces legal and compliance risk.
- Aligns with SmartPulse's educational mission.

### Negative

- Some users expecting "buy/sell signals" may be disappointed.
- Marketing must clearly communicate SmartPulse's role as an intelligence assistant rather than an automated trading system.

# SmartPulse

SmartPulse is an AI-powered institutional market intelligence platform for retail traders.

The platform combines Smart Money Concepts (SMC), ICT methodologies, market structure analysis, liquidity intelligence, AI-generated market narratives, and trader performance analytics to help traders make better trading decisions.

## Vision

To provide institutional-grade market intelligence and performance analytics to retail traders through a modern, scalable SaaS platform.

## Core Features

* AI Market Narratives
* Market Structure Analysis
* Liquidity Intelligence
* Confidence Scoring Engine
* Economic News Awareness
* SmartPulse IQ™
* Automated Trade Journal
* Trader Performance Analytics
